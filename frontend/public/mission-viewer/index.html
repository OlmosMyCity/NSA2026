    Gateway NRHO

    9:2 Near-Rectilinear Halo Orbit around L2 (6.56-day period; perilune ≈ 3,250 km above the north pole, apolune ≈ 71,000 km above the south pole), computed with periodic_orbits.find_nrho(). It is periodic in the rotating frame: toggle with 2 to see it fixed.

    South Pole Descent (HLS)

    Shortly before NRHO perilune, HLS performs DOI: an ellipse dropping to 15 km perilune half an orbit later. At PDI, powered descent begins using E-guidance (braking → high gate → approach → low gate at 100 m → vertical descent at 1 m/s), integrated in the Moon-fixed frame with GRAIL GRGM1200A gravity up to degree 100. Click PDI or Touchdown on the timeline to jump to the landing site (86.8° S). Near the Moon, velocity is measured relative to its surface.

    Reference Frames

    • ECI J2000: inertial, Earth-centered. Used for numerical integration.
    • Earth–Moon Rotating: rotates with the Moon (angle θ = n·t + φ₀ in its plane), keeping it fixed on the x-axis at 384,400 km.
    • Selenocentric: ECI axes centered at the Moon: r − rMoon(t).

    The timeline is non-linear: each phase occupies width proportional to the logarithm of its duration. Scale: 1 unit = 1,000 km. The scene recenters on the focused body (floating origin) to preserve Float32 precision. Spacecraft are not to scale (otherwise invisible): their size scales with the nearest body while remaining smaller. Data: scripts/export_mission.py → data/mission.json.
